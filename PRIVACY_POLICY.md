# BattleCalc — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data BattleCalc collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data. BattleCalc is an unofficial, community-made Discord bot and is **not affiliated with Midjiwan AB** or The Battle of Polytopia.

## 2. Data We Collect

When you interact with BattleCalc, we may collect and store the following data:

**Automatically collected from Discord interactions:**

- Discord user IDs
- Discord usernames (at time of interaction)
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Channel IDs where commands are used
- Command inputs and parameters
- Timestamps of interactions

**Stored for bot functionality:**

- Server whitelist status (whether your server is authorised to use the bot)
- Game session data (score entries, turn-by-turn data submitted during active score-counting sessions)
- Command usage logs (for debugging and improving the bot)

## 3. Data We Do Not Collect

BattleCalc does **not** collect:

- Message content outside of slash command interactions
- Direct messages
- IP addresses or geolocation data
- Voice or video data
- Data from other bots
- Any information from users who do not directly interact with BattleCalc

BattleCalc does not have the Discord Message Content intent enabled and cannot read general server messages.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

Data collected is used solely for:

- Providing bot functionality (calculations, score tracking, session management)
- Enforcing server whitelist access controls
- Debugging errors and improving the bot
- Preventing abuse or misuse of the bot

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Your data is not used for advertising, analytics, or marketing purposes.

Data may be disclosed only if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on secured private infrastructure. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Game session data** is retained for the duration of the game session and may be deleted after the session ends.
- **Command usage logs** are retained for up to 90 days for debugging purposes and then deleted.
- **Server whitelist data** is retained as long as your server has access to the bot.
- **User IDs and interaction records** are retained as long as necessary to provide the service and then deleted upon request or when no longer needed.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Request access** to the data we hold about you
- **Request deletion** of your data — contact us and we will delete your data within 30 days
- **Request correction** of any inaccurate data
- **Opt out** by removing the bot from your server, which will stop all future data collection from that server

To exercise any of these rights, contact **vouchful** on Discord.

## 9. Children's Privacy

BattleCalc is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through the bot, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 10. International Users

BattleCalc is operated from Singapore. If you are accessing the bot from the European Union or other regions with data protection laws, please be aware that your data may be transferred to and processed in Singapore. By using BattleCalc, you consent to this transfer. If you are an EU resident, you may exercise your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) by contacting us.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of BattleCalc after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to make a data request, please contact **vouchful** on Discord.
